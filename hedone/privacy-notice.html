Privacy Notice

Last updated: November 17, 2026 · Applies to the Hedone Fresh website and student membership

This notice explains what personal information we collect when you register, why we collect it, how we protect it, how long we keep it, and who to contact. We follow the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act 10173) of the Philippines.

The short version
  • We collect only what we need to create your student membership and keep your account safe.
  • Your password is never stored as readable text, and your contact number is stored encrypted.
  • We do not sell your information, and we do not collect payment card details when you register.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time (see section 5).

3. How we protect it

  • Your password is stored only as a one-way scrambled value (a hash), so nobody, including our staff, can read it.
  • Your security answers are scrambled in the same way, and your contact number is encrypted.
  • Forms are protected against forged requests, and the database is accessed only through safe, parameterized queries.
  • Repeated failed log-ins are slowed down or blocked, and log-ins are confirmed with a one-time code sent to your email.
  • Only staff who need the information for their work can access it, and the database account used by the website cannot edit or delete our security log.
  • Secret settings are stored outside the public part of the website, and security events are recorded in a log.
  • If a breach affects your information, we will tell you and the National Privacy Commission within 72 hours.

No system is perfectly secure. Choose a strong, unique password and do not share your login.

We do not sell your information. We share it only with service providers that host our system or send our emails, with your school or participating stores when needed to confirm your student discount, and with authorities when the law requires it.

You must be at least 18 years old to register. Registration is rejected if your birthdate shows you are younger.
